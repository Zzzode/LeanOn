LeanOn

Bottom-tab information architecture, guest-first. Five peer destinations; settings & account live in Me.

First-level destinations

  • Today — at-a-glance status
  • Diary — log food, net calories
  • Progress — trends & milestones
  • Partner — two-person support
  • Me — profile, goals, settings

Key rules

  • Logging actions sit inside their screen (+ / FAB)
  • Sign-in is optional, surfaced only in Me
  • Language & units in Me → Settings
  • Each tab keeps its own navigation stack
